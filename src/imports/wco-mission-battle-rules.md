Battle of the Bars: Rules of Engagement Framework (Draft)
Purpose: updated judging criteria and rules of engagement for Battle of the Bars freestyle and static/power categories.

I. Overview
The purpose of this framework is to modernize and clarify the judging criteria for Battle of the Bars, ensuring that athletes are rewarded for complete performance ability, innovation, and execution quality. The competition will feature two distinct (1 v 1) Battle formats:
Freestyle Battles


Static & Power Battles


Each format evaluates athletes across the key facets of calisthenics while recognizing the nuances of style, flow, and creativity that make the sport unique.

II. FREESTYLE BATTLES
Objective: Identify the most complete freestyle athlete — one who demonstrates mastery across all aspects of calisthenics: statics, dynamics, combinations, and power dynamics.
Judging Categories
Statics


Control, strength, and aesthetic of static holds (planche, front lever, handstand variations, etc.)


Difficulty and execution of each static element


Transition smoothness and combination quality between holds


Inclusion of balance-based skills (handstands, one-arm holds) adds value but is weighted slightly lower than maximal strength statics.


Dynamics


Swing-based and explosive elements performed on high bar, p-bars, or floor.


Evaluation based on difficulty, execution, amplitude, landing control, and flow integration.


Super moves (e.g., 720s, 900s) and creative transitions carry higher value when executed cleanly.


Power Dynamics


Strength-based dynamic skills derived from static power movements (planche push-ups, front lever pull-ups, pelican, Van Gelder, etc.).


Judged by difficulty, execution, and power displayed in each repetition.


Emphasis on explosiveness and control in both ascent and descent phases.


Combinations & Flow


The defining element of freestyle performance.


Evaluates the athlete’s ability to link elements across multiple subcategories:


Dynamic combinations: Connection and rhythm between multiple dynamic tricks.


Static combinations: Sequencing of multiple static holds and transitions.


Static-to-dynamic transitions: The ability to flow seamlessly between holds and explosive movements.


Overall flow: Cohesion, rhythm, and aesthetic of the routine as a whole. Two athletes can perform the same moves, yet one will flow more naturally — that quality is recognized.


Creativity


Originality, risk-taking, and unique transitions that differentiate routines.


New move variations, unconventional grips, and surprising transitions earn higher marks.


Use of Area


Effective use of the entire setup: high bar, p-bars, floor, and parallettes.


Points for variety and adaptability across apparatuses.


Offense & Defense (Battle Dynamics)


Offense: Taking initiative, setting the tone with aggressive, high-impact moves or combinations.


Defense: Responding with equal or higher difficulty, intensity, or originality.


Interaction and adaptability within a battle setting are valued — not just executing a pre-set routine.


Performance Considerations
Difficulty: Based on the skill level and complexity of each move and combination.


Execution: Precision, form, and control under fatigue.


Combination Difficulty: Evaluates the challenge of linking multiple high-difficulty moves without pauses.


Risk & Improvisation: Bonuses for spontaneous creativity and real-time adaptation.


Readiness: Athletes must begin their set promptly after the opponent finishes. Minor preparation (chalking, setup) within reason is acceptable to ensure optimal performance.


Judging Panel
3 judges, each specializing in overall FreeStyle:
Statics


Dynamics


Power Dynamics


Combinations/Flow



III. STATIC & POWER BATTLES
Objective: Reward the athlete who demonstrates maximal strength, control, and endurance through static and power-dynamic elements.
Judging Categories
Statics


Difficulty and execution of holds (planche, front lever, Maltese, inverted cross, etc.).


Combination quality — how well multiple holds are connected.


Grip and apparatus variation add value and showcase versatility.


Emphasis on clean lines, full lockouts, and stable control.


Power Dynamics


Strength-based dynamic skills built from static power positions (planche push-ups, full-range front lever pull-ups, etc.).


Judged by difficulty, execution, and explosiveness.


Emphasis on maintaining control, amplitude, and precision across repetitions.


Combinations


Length and complexity of sequences linking static and power dynamic elements.


Value increases with smooth transitions and minimal resting between elements.


Athletes who maintain continuous tension and form throughout combinations are rewarded.


Creativity


Innovative transitions, grip variations, and use of different apparatuses.


Original setups and routines add to the overall score.


Performance Considerations
Difficulty & Control: Quality of each element and the ability to hold or complete moves under fatigue.


Execution: Clean positions, no form breaks, and visible control from start to finish.


Combination Endurance: Value given to extended combinations without cuts or resets.


Setup Time: Athletes may take slightly more time to prepare for safety and optimal performance.


Judging Panel
3 judges specializing in:
Statics


Power Dynamics


Combinations


Execution/Control



IV. Summary
This framework aims to reinforce Battle of the Bars as the premier global calisthenics event by emphasizing complete athleticism, creativity, and execution quality. It rewards not only raw strength and difficulty but also artistry, flow, and battle readiness — ensuring that athletes are recognized as true performers across every facet of the sport.
